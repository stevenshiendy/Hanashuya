# Hanashuya site and member card

Landing page and digital loyalty card for Hanashuya, a choux pastry booth at Papaya Fresh Gallery, Bandung (owner: Glenn, `stevenshiendy`). Copy on every page is Indonesian. The site addresses customers as "Kakak"; the member app uses "Anda" in forms, and the two legal pages use formal "Anda" and "Kami" with defined parties.

## How it works

- Supabase project `jjdsoqpdiksosfotghwi`. All app logic lives in Postgres functions called by RPC; tables have RLS with no direct access.
- Everyone logs in with username or WhatsApp number plus password. Each account has a hidden email `u-<hex>@member.hanashuya.id`; `login_handle()` maps username/number to it. Supabase Auth "Confirm email" must be off.
- Owner account is created once with `select public._setup_owner(name, username, phone, password);` in the SQL Editor. Staff are added from the owner dashboard.
- Forgot password: `RESET_VIA = 'manual'`. The member messages Hanashuya on WhatsApp; the owner makes a 6-digit code in Bantuan akun and sends it with a wa.me link. The automatic WhatsApp Cloud API route is parked for later.
- Card QR is a 5-minute signed token (`card_token()`; the current and previous window scan); the cashier scans it. Rules: 1 stamp per Rp50.000, no receipt number at the counter (the cashier enters only the total; the same card and total again within 10 minutes is refused as a double tap; Cocokkan struk pairs each stamp with one VireoPOS receipt of the same total within 15 minutes and flags leftovers and different customer names, see `_match_stamps`), no daily cap (a card that gets more than `max_stamps_per_day` in a day is only flagged `banyak_stempel_sehari` for the owner), staff can't stamp their own card, typed codes and number searches are flagged (`kode_diketik`, `nomor_dicari`; every number search is in `audit_log` as `cari_nomor`), a number search shows the full name so the cashier asks "Atas nama siapa Kak?" before using it, undo needs owner approval, everything is in `audit_log`. Purchase time is the stamp time.
- Rewards: stamp 3 diskon 10%, stamp 7 diskon 20%, stamp 10 gratis 1 produk. One QR for everything: the member shows the card QR, the cashier asks and taps Pakai (`staff_redeem_reward`, using the scan ticket), then enters DISKONSTAMP1 / DISKONSTAMP2 or tukar poin in VireoPOS (vouchers: NEWMEMBER50 and BIRTHDAYGIFT, `VOUCHER_STEP` in staff.js). While the QR is open the member app checks every 3 seconds and shows "Hadiah dipakai". `member_redeem` now refuses: members can't use a reward on their own.
- Card cycles: a full card (10 stamps) stays on screen until all its rewards are used; extra stamps wait (`waiting`) and carry over to the next card. `card_cycles` stores one row per filled card (`filled_at`, `closed_at` once all rewards are used), kept up to date by `_sync_cycles` after every sale, redemption and approved undo; `_card_view` gives the card shown (`cycle`, `position`, `waiting`, `cards_done`). Meant as the base for tiered rewards later (for example, cards closed per member).
- Vouchers (`_vouchers`): `welcome` = Diskon 50% untuk 1 produk, valid from sign-up day to +30 days; `ulang_tahun` = 1 box Mini Choux isi 8, valid H-7 to H+7 around the birthday, once a year, only if the member joined 30+ days before that birthday (29 Feb becomes 28 Feb). They show in the member's Kartu and Hadiah tabs and in the cashier's ready list; the cashier taps Pakai (`staff_use_voucher`). Uses are rows in `redemptions` with `kind` = 'welcome' / 'ulang_tahun' (cycle = birthday year, slot 0), so undo, Hari ini and backups cover them. Stamp-reward queries filter `kind = 'stempel'`. Members can't change their birth date once set; the owner fixes it in Bantuan akun (`owner_set_birth_date`).
- Member app header: back arrow + logo link to the landing page. WhatsApp help (`waLink` in lib.js, number in config.js) on the login screen, under the card and in Akun.
- The username is optional everywhere except the self sign-up form: `whoami().profile_complete` no longer needs it, and `update_my_profile` keeps the current one (or none) when left empty.
- Sign-up asks for full name, birth date (17+), WhatsApp number, username, password, optional gender, and an optional WhatsApp promo box (no terms box while the legal pages are offline). `consent_log` records promo choices (append-only).
- Staying signed in: supabase-js keeps the login in localStorage, and `api/session.js` (a Vercel function) keeps the refresh token in a 400-day HttpOnly cookie on `/api/session`, renewed on every open and cleared on Keluar. `session()` in lib.js restores from it when Safari has wiped localStorage (7 days without a visit). The member card is installable to the home screen (`member/manifest.webmanifest`, icons `img/icon-192.png` / `icon-512.png`). The local preview server has no `/api`, so the restore call 404s there quietly.
- Cashiers tap gender and age range for walk-ins; members fill in their own.
- Cards made by the cashier: `_create_login` takes an optional `p_meta` (birth date, gender, promo choice) for `_handle_new_user`; `staff_create_member` sets the transaction setting `hanashuya.signup_method` so the promo consent row says `daftar kasir` instead of `daftar`, and logs `daftar_lewat_kasir`. `_open_card` (shared by QR/code lookup, number search and cashier sign-up) makes the scan ticket; `scan_tickets.via` and `sales.via` take `qr`, `kode`, `nomor`, `daftar`.
- Printed voucher codes (10 Nov 2026, first use: the Papaya cashier voucher): `voucher_batches` (title, label, VireoPOS `pos_code` such as MANIS, `ends_on`, `one_per_member`) and `voucher_codes` (8 characters from the card-code alphabet, one row per printed voucher, `claimed_by`). Each voucher's QR is `/v/<code>`, which `vercel.json` redirects to `/member/?v=<code>`; member.js keeps the code in localStorage (`hs-voucher-code`) until the member is signed in, then `claim_voucher_code` saves it. Scanning never claims, so an unfinished sign-up just scans again. Hadiah has a "Punya voucher kertas?" box. The cashier saves a code from the card chip (typed, or the voucher QR scanned before or after the member QR; `staff_claim_code`). Claimed codes appear in `_vouchers` as kind `kode` (period = code id) and are used with `staff_use_voucher`, step text from `pos_code`. Wrong codes from the app are limited to 5 an hour (`kode_salah` in audit_log; `_claim_code` returns a status instead of raising so the try is logged). The owner sees printed / saved / used per batch and moves or frees a code in Bantuan akun > Voucher kertas (`owner_code_report`, `owner_move_code`). Codes are loaded with the SQL file made with each print run.
- Testing SQL without Supabase: the schema runs in PGlite (in-memory Postgres) (`npm i @electric-sql/pglite` in a scratch folder; there's no Postgres on the Mac), with stubs for `auth.users`, `auth.identities`, `auth.uid()` (reads a `test.uid` setting) and the pgcrypto functions, and the `create extension pgcrypto` line removed. `_demo-kasir.html` (untracked, not deployed) runs the real kasir page on a fake backend in `_demo/lib.js`; its demo member number is 0812 3456 7890.
- Standalone from VireoPOS. Cocokkan struk imports the VireoPOS Laporan Penjualan Rinci (.xls via SheetJS, or .csv) to check stamps against receipts. Importing VireoPOS members is a later, one-time job.
